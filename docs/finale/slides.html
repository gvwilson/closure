Conclusion

Share What You Learned

  • This guide was created because nothing like it existed
    • It is incomplete, and your help is welcome
  • Share what you have learned
    • Give talks
    • Post on social media, preferably Mastodon
    • The big platforms are single points of institutional failure
  • Share what has not worked as well
    • So others do not find those landmines the hard way
  • Sharing during difficult times protects your mental health
  • Take time to grieve
    • The end of years of work hurts, even when voluntary
    • Colleagues may be suffering too
    • Others may have survivors’ guilt
  • Remember that people become altruistic and brave after disaster

Organize

  • The best preventive action is to join professional associations
    • Push them to take meaningful action
    • Software has few meaningful associations to join
  • This makes many people uncomfortable
    • Membership in voluntary organizations has declined
    • We are out of practice
  • The bad guys are not
    • An anti-vax conspiracy theorist is more likely to run for school board than an epidemiologist
  • Right now the focus is preserving what we have
    • But playing defense is just a way to lose more slowly
